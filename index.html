About me

I turn business complexity into digital solutions that work.

I'm a digital transformation and automation professional with 10 years of experience across technology, consulting, financial services, utilities and fleet mobility.

My work sits at the intersection of business, data and technology. I take complex or fragmented processes, understand the business need behind them, and translate them into practical solutions through process design, automation, analytics and digital products.

My experience started in business analysis and customer experience, expanded into business intelligence and data, and has evolved into designing end-to-end digital solutions using Microsoft Power Platform, automation and AI. That progression means I'm equally comfortable gathering requirements with business stakeholders, modelling data, designing an operating process, building automated workflows, and creating the reporting needed to measure whether the solution is working.

Today, I focus on solutions that make operations more scalable, visible and controlled, whether that means automating manual work, improving product adoption, strengthening financial controls, creating better decision-making tools, or making organizational knowledge easier to access through AI.

My work has evolved from reporting what is happening to building systems that improve what happens next.
